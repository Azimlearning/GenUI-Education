# stimuli.md — environmental variables

External pressures that trigger mutation. Append freely between rounds.

- S1. Malaysian students revise on phones with metered data, often shared.
- S2. SPM is the single behavioural driver. Anything not obviously exam-relevant loses to a past-year paper.
- S3. Study materials spread through WhatsApp groups, not app stores or logins.
- S4. School networks are locked down, often without usable Wi-Fi, and school PCs may be offline.
- S5. Rural and B40 schools lack functioning physical labs, which is the original problem statement.
- S6. Per-token model costs keep falling; small local models keep improving. Anything expensive today may be cheap by the time it ships.
- S7. Teachers are the highest-leverage distribution channel and the least likely to install anything.
- S8. Generated content that is subtly wrong is worse than no content, because a memorised wrong model persists.
