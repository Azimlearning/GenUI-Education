**What went well during the event?**
> The food and pre-event arrangements were very good. The judges themselves were professional and engaged — they even came to ask us questions after the event had ended. The committee also tried to help us find a charging port when we ran into power issues.

**What most needs improvement?** *(required)*
> The presentation format was inconsistent and unfair. The original format (2-phase judging: all groups present, then top 10 chosen to present in front, then top 5 winners) was supposed to run in order of form submission — which itself was fine as just a queue order. But in practice the actual calling order kept changing randomly throughout the day (we were told we were group 22, then later 40, then suddenly other groups were called ahead of us out of nowhere). This made it very hard to plan bathroom breaks, prayer breaks, or any short break at all, since we always had to stay alert in case our group's name was called. On top of the ordering issue, the judging rules kept shifting: the first few groups got 10 minutes not including Q&A and presented to two separate judge panels, then it changed to presenting to all judges at once, then the time limit started including Q&A, then Q&A was dropped entirely, and the time kept shrinking until our group had only 3 minutes total. Teams that presented earlier had a clear advantage in time, Q&A, demo, and judge attention, and unsurprisingly the top 5 winners were all early presenters. Beyond judging, the event needs: working venue Wi-Fi (we ran on personal hotspots the entire event), enough charging stations so people don't have to leave laptops unattended in other teams' areas, clear upfront communication of all submission requirements and the deliverables QR code process, better time management (the event ran until 6:30pm instead of the scheduled 5pm and still skipped the planned top-10 phase), no last-minute venue changes mid-presentation, and a properly run closing ceremony with a dedicated emcee/floor manager and correct winning team names on the slides.
